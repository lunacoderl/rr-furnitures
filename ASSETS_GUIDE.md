# RR FURNITURES — ASSET DROP ZONE GUIDE

Welcome! This folder structure is fully prepared for you to add your official photographs, videos, and logo assets for **RR Furnitures**.

---

## 📁 Where to Add Your Files

You can drop your media files directly into `public/` (recommended for static serving):

### 1. Logo
**Path:** `public/logo/` or `src/assets/logo/`
- `logo.svg` / `logo.png` (Main RR Furnitures logo / RR Monogram)
- `logo-white.svg` / `logo-white.png` (White/light version for dark backgrounds)
- `favicon.svg` / `favicon.ico`

### 2. Sofa Photographs
**Path:** `public/images/sofas/` (8 initial sofa photos have already been organized here!)
- `sofawork-01.png` to `sofawork-04.png`
- `sofaworks-01.png` to `sofaworks-04.png`
- Any additional sofa photos (Sectionals, L-shapes, Mustard, Blue, Burgundy, Beige, Grey, Rose, Sofa-cum-beds)

### 3. Workshop & Craft Photos
**Path:** `public/images/workshop/`
- Photos of frame building, timber joinery, spring/webbing lacing, foam cutting, and master upholstery tailoring
- `workshop-01.jpg`, `workshop-02.jpg`, etc.

### 4. Showroom & Store Photos
**Path:** `public/images/store/`
- Photos of the store exterior, showroom entrance, or Rikshala Bazar shop front
- `store-exterior.jpg`, `showroom-interior.jpg`

### 5. Videos
**Path:** `public/videos/`
- Short cinematic videos of sofa craft in motion or finished furniture walkthroughs
- Recommended formats: `.mp4`, `.webm`
- Example names: `craft-in-motion.mp4`, `hero-reel.mp4`
- Video poster thumbnail: `video-poster.jpg`

---

## 💡 Note
All data schemas in `src/data/` dynamically reference these paths. Whenever you add or rename files, they will automatically integrate across the Home, About, Services, Works, and Contact pages!
